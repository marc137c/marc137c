# Viggo's Barbershop: specification

This page holds the owner's decisions in force (D) and the rules the November 2026 fix plan adds (R). Each row names the test that enforces it. **now** means that test exists and runs in `npm test`, which the Netlify build runs before every deploy. **step N** means plan step N adds the enforcement; a planned file name may change, and the step that adds the test updates its row and sets it to **now**. A decision changes only with the owner, and the page, the test and the code change in the same commit. `test/docs/spec.test.mjs` checks this page.

**Privacy facts (D6).** These are the only facts `personvern.html` may state.

- **Controller:** Viggos Barbershop Kurti, org.nr 937 845 812, Violgata 22, 1776 Halden, phone 409 63 911, kurtivigan1@gmail.com.
- **Data:** name, phone number, and the chosen service, add-ons and time.
- **Purpose:** the booking, plus the barber's notifications and calendar.
- **Legal basis:** GDPR art. 6(1)(b).
- **Processors and recipients:** Netlify for hosting, functions and storage (Netlify Blobs; Netlify, Inc., USA), the only booking store. The barber's notification (name, service and time, never the phone number) travels encrypted through his browser's push service: Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft. If the barber subscribes his phone calendar, his calendar service (iCloud or Google Calendar) fetches name, phone, service and time. Google: the map (loaded only after a click) and, when the reviews section shows Google data, the reviewers' photos; the reviews themselves are fetched server-side. Instagram (Meta): post images, only when the feed has posts. The fonts are self-hosted since step 6 (F47), so Google no longer serves fonts; this replaces the earlier "Google for fonts".
- **Abuse counters:** times of recent attempts under a keyed hash of the IP address, phone or login, never the value itself; deleted by the daily clean-up once the newest attempt is more than 24 hours old, so at the latest 48 hours after it.
- **Visitor statistics:** per Oslo day, page views per page (front page, personvern) and per source group (Instagram, Facebook, Google, TikTok, direct, other). No cookies, no IP address, no ID, no browser data, no referrer address; nothing is stored on the visitor's device. Only the published site counts; bots and previews are skipped. Kept 400 days, at Netlify (functions and Blobs).
- **On the device:** only the visitor's own language choice (localStorage `lang`), when English is chosen.
- **Retention:** bookings are deleted 90 days after the appointment (by the daily clean-up, so at the latest 91 days after the appointment date). Every period stated on the page is its worst case: the threshold plus one clean-up interval (R30).
- **Rights:** the visitor's GDPR rights, including the right to complain to Datatilsynet.

## Defaults for the open owner questions

The owner answers these once, in the final yes before going live. Until then the plan builds the default.

- **Per-phone booking cap:** 6 per 24 h.
- **Hero rating badge:** shown whenever Google reports at least 5 reviews, whatever the score. A low score is never hidden. Built in step 5 (`HERO_MIN` in `viggos-barbershop/reviews.js`, `test/page/site-widgets.test.mjs`).

Built in step 5: with no posts the feed and its lead are hidden, and with no review text the card list is hidden (`test/page/site-widgets.test.mjs`).
